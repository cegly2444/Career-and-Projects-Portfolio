About Me

Hello, my name is Christopher! I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at UIUC focused on robotics, electro-mechanical systems and aerospace. I enjoy digging into the nitty gritty details of engineering design work. Piecing apart a problem into its most basic components and learing as I go is the most satisfying part of my work. More specifically I believe its best to know how to ask the right question rather than knowing how to do the next thing. Its this process of learning and exploring that makes me who I am!

Past the control scripts and hand calculations, I have many non-academic passions too! Such as: exploring the outdoors, playing music, cooking with friends, traveling, trying new foods, and staying active!
