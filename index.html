Low-Cost Custom 6-Axis Robotic Arm

Designed, fabricated, and programmed a fully custom 6-DOF robotic arm from scratch, featuring 3D-printed links, custom electronics, and inverse kinematics control via a Python backend.

SLAM Golf Putter

Integrated real-time SLAM mapping, gradient descent navigation, and computer vision to locate and putt a golf ball into a goal.

Multi-Scheme Control for Robotic Arm

Implemented multiple feedback control schemes (impedance control, friction compensation, and task space control) to navigate a robotic arm through a complex obstacle course.

6-Legged Robotic Walker

Synthesized planar four-bar linkages in CAD to optimize foot trajectory patterns for a hexapod walking gait, then fabricated and demonstrated the physical walking robot.

Low-Budget Gear-Shifting Robotic Car

Designed a dog-clutch transmission with 4 forward gears and reverse, controlled by 2 servos and an Arduino script.
